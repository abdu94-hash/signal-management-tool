### How to read this log

Each entry gives the date, the file, what changed, and whether the change affects a value a reader
may already have relied on. A change marked **material** alters a number, a threshold, a formula, or
a regulatory statement, and a copy taken before that date should be replaced. A change marked
**editorial** affects wording or layout only.
